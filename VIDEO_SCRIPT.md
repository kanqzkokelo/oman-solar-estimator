# Demo Video Script — 90 seconds

Record with OBS or the browser's built-in screen recorder. 1080p, browser fullscreen, light mode.
Narration is a guide — say it naturally in your own words.

---

## Shot 1 — Hook (0–10s)
**Screen:** Home page, hero visible.
**Voice:** "Oman has some of the best sunshine on earth — 5.5 to 6.5 kilowatt-hours per square metre every day. Yet most people can't tell you what solar would do for their roof. This tool answers that in seconds."

## Shot 2 — The trace (10–30s)
**Screen:** Wizard. Click **Demo roof**, watch the polygon snap onto the ISM campus. Point at the area readout (459 m², ~64 panels).
**Voice:** "Trace your roof on the satellite map — this is the Indian School Muscat campus, pre-loaded as the demo zone. Area, usable space and panel count update instantly — 460 square metres traces to about 130 panels."

## Shot 3 — Details (30–45s)
**Screen:** Continue → property details. Flip bill/kWh mode, change governorate, show the live summary side panel.
**Voice:** "Enter your property type, bill or consumption, governorate and dust level — the live summary updates as you go."

## Shot 4 — Results (45–70s)
**Screen:** Calculate. Slow pan over: 3D roof scene, system size / generation / savings / payback cards, monthly generation-vs-consumption chart, loss breakdown, environmental impact, SDG 7 + SDG 11 + Net Zero 2050 badges.
**Voice:** "Within seconds: a full business case — recommended system, annual generation, savings, payback, CO₂ avoided, and how it feeds Oman's Net Zero 2050 goal."

## Shot 5 — The offline money shot (70–85s)
**Screen:** Turn off Wi-Fi. Refresh the page. Trace the demo roof again → imagery still there, estimate still works.
**Voice:** "And because this engine is built on real Oman data — governorate irradiance, Nama tariffs, desert dust, Gulf heat — it's engineered to survive the real world. I'll prove it: Wi-Fi off, and the satellite map, the trace and the estimate all still work. That's a solar feasibility study that never depends on a connection."

## Shot 6 — Outro (85–90s)
**Screen:** PDF report opens.
**Voice:** "An Oman-specific solar feasibility study in under a minute — aligned with SDG 7, SDG 11 and Vision 2040."

---

### Recording tips
- Rehearse once with the script tap-tap-tap timing; fix UI pacing (count to 2 before clicking).
- If narration feels stiff, record a silent take at 1.5× and add captions in CapCut/Clipchamp.
- The Wi-Fi toggle is the single most memorable moment — keep it.
- Export MP4, ~15–30 MB.
