# DEMO CARD — print, fold, keep by keyboard

## OPENING (10s)
"I built a tool where you trace your roof on a satellite map and it tells you your solar
savings — system size, generation, bill savings, payback, CO2 — using real Omani data:
per-governorate sun, monthly temperatures, the official 2025 Nama tariffs, and desert dust."

## DEMO LOOP (90s)
1. Search Salalah / click city → fly in.
2. "Trace my roof" → corners → Demo roof → area + governorate auto-detect.
3. Step 2: type bill in OMR → auto-converts to kWh ("users enter a bill, not energy units").
4. Calculate → read top row: system size · generation · savings · payback.
5. Toggle Business → "see how the numbers change for commercial".
6. Toggle dust Low→High → "sensitivity, live".
7. PDF report.

## THE 3 SCRIPTS
**"Did AI build this?"** → "Heavy AI assistance — the concept, the Omani data research and every
product decision are mine. I reviewed every line and can walk you through anything in the code."

**"17-year payback?"** → "Honest numbers. The economics are driven by high consumers and
commercial users on the flat 25-baisa tariff; the 14-baisa first tier is among the world's
cheapest power. Real fit: installers for lead-gen, awareness partners like Nama."

**"How accurate?"** → "~1,495 kWh per kWp per year in Muscat, 67% performance ratio — matches
published figures for Oman. Engineering estimate for planning, not a promise — the report has
a disclaimer."

## MISSION CLOSE (10s)
"Free is the point — it's a public-good tool. It advances SDG 7 and SDG 11 and Oman's Net Zero
2050: a 16 kWp system avoids ~15 t CO2/yr — ~700 trees or 3 cars off the road, every year."

## CRASH PLAN (say, then continue)

## NEVER SAY
- "the tariffs are fetched dynamically" → say "official APSR rates, effective 1 Jan 2025, in the engine as constants"
- "I wired all the APIs" → "AI did the code, I did the data and design"
- "predict future weather" → "long-term climate averages — reliable for a typical year"
